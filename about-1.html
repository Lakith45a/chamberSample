About Us

The Premier Voice of Sri Lanka’s Private Sector

We’re here to support businesses and be their collective voice in Sri Lanka. Our diverse membership includes everyone from large corporations to small businesses, and we work closely with government institutions to help businesses thrive.

We harness our extensive networks to provide a wide range of services to help businesses grow. This includes promoting international trade and investment, sharing valuable market insights, offering educational sessions and workshops, advocating for businesses with the government, and providing essential business services like issuing Certificates of Origin, all aimed at boosting the country’s economy.

We actively engage with government officials, the President, Prime Minister, and relevant Ministers, and collaborate with industry experts, economists, thought leaders, professionals and policy advocates and operate specialised committees to help shape national policies.

Additionally, our flagship events like the Sri Lanka Economic Summit and Sri Lanka Investment Forum are vital in fostering discussions on socio-economic policies and promoting a sustainable and progressive economy.

Our Vision

To be the benchmark Chamber of Commerce in the Asia Pacific region and the driving force in national economic and social development

Our Mission

To be the most influential and effective voice of the private sector in promoting an environment conducive to national economic development within a framework of sustainable social equity

Structure

The affairs of the Chamber are administered by a Committee of the Chamber, with members elected or appointed in compliance with the Chamber Rules.

The Board functions as a sub Committee of the Chamber vested with powers delegated by the Committee.

The Chamber Secretariat headed by a Secretary General who is also its Chief Executive Officer and a member of the Board, functions to implement the programmes of the Chamber.
