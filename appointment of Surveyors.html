Appointment of Surveyors & Assessors

The Ceylon Chamber appoint panels of surveyors annually to carry out surveys in accordance with the requirements of local & foreign buyers for various commodities and products, as well as for surveying of ships.

01

Commodities & Products

Comprehensive inspection of export and import merchandise, including agricultural produce, Ceylon tea, spices, rubber, and industrial products to guarantee quality, weight, and grade compliance.

  • Quality & Grade Verification
  • Quantity & Weight Certification
  • Packing & Pre-shipment Audit
02

Surveying of Ships

Specialized marine surveys for international vessels, cargo condition monitoring, draft surveys, hatch inspections, bunker surveys, and marine damage evaluations conducted by maritime experts.

  • Draft & Bunker Surveys
  • Vessel Condition & Seaworthiness
  • Cargo Loading & Discharge Supervision
03

Local & Foreign Standards

Empanelled annually, our licensed panels of independent surveyors and assessors adhere strictly to international trade regulations, LC specifications, and bespoke buyer requirements.

  • Annually Appointed Panel of Experts
  • Internationally Recognized Reports
  • Impartial & Legally Accepted Assessments
Process Workflow

How the Survey Process Works

Streamlined procedure to commission an official Ceylon Chamber accredited survey

1. Submit Request

Provide details of the commodity, shipment, vessel, or required survey criteria to the Chamber Secretariat.

2. Panel Appointment

The Chamber assigns qualified, independent surveyors from the official annual panel tailored to the domain.

3. Certified Report

Comprehensive on-site inspection is executed and an authoritative, globally accepted certificate is issued.
